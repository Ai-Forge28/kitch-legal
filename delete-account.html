Delete your Kitch account

You can delete your account and its data inside the app in under a minute, or by emailing aiforge.28@gmail.com if you can no longer open the app. This page applies to the Kitch app by AI Forge LLC.

Option 1: delete in the app

  1. Open Kitch and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning, enter your password and tap Delete account.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. If you signed in a long time ago, the app may ask you to sign in again first.

Option 2: request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, send an email to aiforge.28@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Kitch account, with the subject "Delete my Kitch account". We may reply to verify that the account is yours. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days and will confirm by email when done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email and password).
  • Your profile: display name, plan and usage counters, and profile photo.
  • Your private pantry: items, collections, scan history, removed items, shopping trips and saved recipes.
  • Your membership in any group. If you are the only member, the group and everything in it is deleted. If you own a group with other members, ownership passes to another member.
  • Recipes saved on your phone for your account, removed from the app on that device when deletion completes.
