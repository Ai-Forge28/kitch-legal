Kitch Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Kitch collects the account, pantry and receipt data it needs to run the app, sends receipt photos and recipe requests to Google's Gemini AI through our servers, never sells your data, and lets you delete your account inside the app.

1. Who we are

Kitch (the "App") is a mobile app for tracking the food in your pantry and refrigerator, generating recipes with AI, and managing shopping lists. It is operated by AI Forge LLC, an Arizona limited liability company ("AI Forge", "we", "us"). Privacy contact: aiforge.28@gmail.com.

Kitch is intended for people aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account data: email address, password (stored and hashed by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it), and display name.
  • Profile photo (optional): an image you choose from your photo library. It is stored in Firebase Storage and shown to members of any group you join.
  • Pantry and kitchen data: items, quantities, units, categories, storage locations, expiration dates, prices and stores, pantry collections, shopping trips, items you have removed, and saved recipes.
  • Receipt photos: pictures you take with the camera or pick from your library to scan a receipt. The App does not store the image. It is sent to our server for processing (see section 4) and discarded after processing. What is saved is the list of items, store name, prices and total that the scan produced, in your scan history.
  • Recipe requests: what you type when asking for a recipe (for example a craving or custom instructions) together with the names, quantities and remaining days of the pantry items used to build the recipe. We do not store these requests on our servers.
  • Group data: if you create or join a group (up to 3 members), the group's invite code, member list (display name and photo), and the pantry, shopping, removed-item and recipe entries that members share.

Information created automatically

  • Plan and usage counters: your subscription tier and counters of recipes generated and receipts scanned, used to enforce daily limits.
  • Device integrity checks: Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity may confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. Google processes the device attestation; we do not store it.
  • Technical logs: Google Cloud keeps standard server logs of requests to our servers. Our own code does not write your email, pantry data or receipt contents to logs.
  • On-device storage: your theme choice and your generated recipes are saved on your phone and removed when you uninstall the App or delete your account.

What we do not collect: location, contacts, microphone audio, advertising identifiers, analytics or crash-reporting data. Kitch shows no ads and sends no push notifications.

Permissions: the App asks for camera access to photograph receipts and photo-library access to select a receipt or a profile picture. You can revoke either in your phone's settings; the features that need them will stop working.

3. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide the App: store your pantry, track expiration dates, build shopping lists, scan receipts, generate recipes, and run groups.
  • To enforce usage limits, prevent abuse and keep the service secure.
  • To respond to your support or deletion requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Who receives information

Service providers. We use Google to run the App: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Storage, Cloud Functions, Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity. These providers process data on our behalf.

Gemini AI. When you scan a receipt, the photo is sent from the App to our server and from there to the Google Gemini API, which reads it and returns the items. When you request a recipe, your request and the pantry details above are sent the same way. Do not photograph parts of a receipt that show information you would not want processed, such as your full name, address, loyalty number or payment card details.

Kitch uses the Gemini API through a Google Cloud project with billing enabled. Under Google's terms for paid Gemini API services, Google does not use the content we send, or the responses, to improve its products or train its models; it processes that content to provide the service and as described in Google's terms.

AI output can be wrong. See our Terms of Service.

Other Kitch users. Information you put into a group (display name, photo, shared pantry, shopping and recipe entries) is visible to the other members of that group. Your private pantry is visible only to you.

Legal and corporate reasons. We may disclose information if required by law or a valid legal request, to protect the rights, safety or security of users or the service, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, with notice to you where required.

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context advertising.

5. How long we keep information

We keep account and pantry data until you delete it or delete your account. Receipt photos and recipe requests are not kept by the App. Expired entries in your removed-items list are purged automatically. Server logs kept by Google Cloud follow Google's own retention practices.

6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account in the App: Settings > Delete Account. You will be asked for your password. Deletion removes your profile, your private pantry data, collections, shopping trips, scan history, removed items, saved recipes, your profile photo, your group memberships, and your sign-in account. If you are the only member of a group, the group and its data are deleted. If you own a group with other members, ownership passes to another member.

If you cannot use the App, email aiforge.28@gmail.com from the address on your account, or visit our account deletion page. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days of a verified request.

What remains after deletion, and why:

  • Group recipes you shared stay available to the group so it does not lose them, but they are anonymized: your name becomes "Deleted user" and your photo is removed.
  • Shared group entries (pantry items, shopping trips, removed items) stay in the group. They carry an internal account identifier that no longer maps to any profile after deletion.
  • Recipes shared in a group you left earlier may still show your display name until they are removed. Email us and we will anonymize or remove them.
  • Backups and logs may keep residual copies for a limited time before they expire.
  • Information we must keep to comply with law, resolve disputes or enforce our terms.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile and pantry data in the App, delete individual entries, and delete your account. You may also email us to ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information.

  • California and other U.S. state residents: depending on your state, you may have the right to know, access, correct and delete your personal information and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising. Email us to make a request; we may need to verify your identity.
  • Residents of Mexico: you may exercise your rights of access, rectification, cancellation and opposition (ARCO) by emailing us.
  • Residents of the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the United Kingdom: Kitch is not currently offered to you.

8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and is stored with Google Firebase, which applies access controls and encryption. No system is perfectly secure. Use a strong, unique password and keep your device updated. If a breach affecting your information occurs, we will notify you and authorities as the law requires.

9. International processing

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries where Google operates. By using the App you understand your information may be transferred there.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version at the same address with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify you in the App or by email.
